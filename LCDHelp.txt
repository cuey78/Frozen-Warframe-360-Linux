Preview Box

The Preview Box is synchronized with the Display.
You can use the mouse to drag the information in the Preview Box. After adjusting,
click Save As. You can use the keyboard WASD to adjust the position in units of 1
pixel, and shift+WASD to adjust the position in units of 10 pixels.
For products with large screens, you need to double-click to pop up a large-size
preview box, which can also be edited.
